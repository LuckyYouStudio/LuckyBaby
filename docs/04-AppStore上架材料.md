一家人一起用

## Description (English)
LuckyBaby is a pregnancy journal the whole family shares. Mom records, dad takes part, family stays in the loop.

No community, no ads, no data selling. Open the app and it's just your family.

Together
· Join in a second with an invite code or QR; dad and family use their own phones
· Mom decides who sees each entry: only me, partner, or the whole family
· Dad can check in supplements for her and sign up to join checkups

Checkups without stress
· Standard prenatal milestones scheduled by week: registration, NT scan, screening, anomaly scan, glucose test, NST
· The night before: fasting or not, what to bring
· Photograph reports and keep them with the visit
· Values shown with reference ranges, calmly

One tap a day
· How are you today: okay / threw up / exhausted / unwell
· Folic acid, calcium, iron, DHA reminders; dad can check in for her
· Weight curve, symptoms, mood, kick counter, labor timer, hospital bag list

Designed for expecting moms
· Dark mode for night, larger text for grandparents
· Chinese / English
· Baby's size each week, compared to a fruit

Privacy
· Data visible only to your family
· No advertising or analytics SDKs
· Delete any record, or your account, any time

LuckyBaby is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Reference ranges are for understanding only; follow your doctor.

## 审核备注（App Review Notes）
The app uses anonymous sign-in by default; no account is required to try it. Tap "先用示例家庭看看 / Try a sample family first" on the first screen to load sample data with all features. Sign in with Apple and email link are optional, used only to restore data on a new phone. Account deletion is on the Family tab. The app is not a medical device; reference ranges are informational.
