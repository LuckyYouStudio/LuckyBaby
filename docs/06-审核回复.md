## 回复正文

Thank you for reviewing LuckyBaby (幸运宝贝). Please find the requested information below. A screen recording captured on an iPhone 15 Pro Max running the latest iOS is attached; it starts from launching the app and covers onboarding, the main features, Sign in with Apple (account creation/login) and account deletion.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
LuckyBaby is a private pregnancy journal for one family. The mom-to-be records checkups, supplements (folic acid, iron, etc.), daily mood/weight/symptoms and, before pregnancy, her menstrual cycle; her partner and close relatives join with an invite code and can see the progress and help (check in on her behalf, offer to accompany her to a checkup, leave comments). It solves the problem that existing pregnancy apps are single-user and full of ads/community content, so the father and grandparents are left out. There is no community, no ads, no e-commerce and no medical diagnosis; the mom-to-be controls what each family member can see.

**3. Setup and accessing the main features**
No account or login is required. On first launch the app creates an anonymous identity on the device automatically.
- Accept the privacy notice → tap "我来建家庭 / Start the family" → enter a name, choose "已怀孕 / Pregnant" (or "备孕中 / Trying to conceive") and a date → "建立家庭 / Create family". You land on the Today tab with the checkup schedule, supplements and family feed pre-filled.
- Family members join on their own phone: "我有邀请码 / I have a code" → enter the 6-character code shown on the creator's Family tab (or scan its QR code). Two devices are needed to see live sync; alternatively tap "先用示例家庭看看 / Try the sample family" at the bottom of the onboarding screen to load demo data and switch between the mom / dad / relative views.
- Optional account linking: Family tab → "用 Apple 绑定 / Link with Apple" (or link with an email magic link). This is the only registration/login in the app and is optional; it exists so data can be restored on a new phone.
- Account deletion: after linking, Family tab → "删除账号 / Delete account" → confirm. This deletes the user's identity and all data they created, server-side.
- Language: the app follows the device language (Chinese / English) and can be switched on the Family tab.
No credentials are needed for review. Test Apple IDs work with Sign in with Apple.

**4. External services**
- Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions) for family data sync, anonymous sign-in, Sign in with Apple and email magic-link, report photo storage and account deletion.
- Sign in with Apple (optional account linking).
- Apple Push Notification service via Expo's push service (optional reminders); local notifications for checkup and supplement reminders.
- No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no payment processor, no AI service, no third-party content providers.

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions identically in all regions. The only regional aspect is language: UI is available in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the device setting. The built-in checkup schedule template reflects common prenatal-visit timing in China but is fully editable and is presented as a reference only.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
LuckyBaby is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat. It is a record-keeping tool. Reference ranges shown next to values (e.g. blood pressure, OGTT) and the ovulation estimate are general public-health information with an explicit note to follow the user's doctor; the fertility estimate is labelled as not suitable for contraception. All content is written by us; there is no protected third-party material. Health data is processed only with the user's separate consent shown on first launch, and can be deleted at any time.
